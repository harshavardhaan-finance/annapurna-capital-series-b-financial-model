## 1. INDUSTRY CONTEXT

**Sector:** Agri-Fintech — Post-Harvest Financing | **Geography:** India

Every year, Indian farmers lose an estimated **₹90,000+ crore** to "distress selling" — selling grain within weeks of harvest at the lowest price of the year, purely because they need cash and have nowhere affordable to store it. India already has a regulatory instrument for this — the **electronic Negotiable Warehouse Receipt (eNWR)** — which lets a farmer deposit produce, get a receipt, and borrow against it while prices recover. **Under 2% of eligible produce is ever financed this way**, for two reasons:

1. **Trust failure** — paper receipts are forgeable, and banks can't verify stored quantity/quality in real time.
2. **Access failure** — even where a trustworthy warehouse exists, it's often too far for a smallholder to reach.

**No company has built the digital, trust-verified, farmer-usable rail that solves both.** That's the gap Annapurna fills.

### 2.1 The Product: "AnnaChain"

Annapurna runs **two warehouse formats**:

| | Partner Warehouses | Owned Mini-Warehouses |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | Third-party owned; Annapurna fits IoT sensors | Annapurna builds and owns directly |
| Capital intensity | Asset-light | Asset-heavy |
| Where deployed | 8 states with existing warehousing | Underpenetrated rural clusters with no adequate warehouse |
| Capacity | Large (5,000–10,000 t) | Small, modular (~700 t average) |
| Revenue to Annapurna | Facilitation commission + SaaS/IoT fee | Facilitation commission + direct storage fee |

**Four technology layers**, applied across both formats:
1. **IoT-Verified Warehousing** — sensors confirm stored quantity/quality in real time
2. **Blockchain-Anchored eNWR Issuance** — WDRA-compliant, tamper-evident digital receipts
3. **Instant Credit Marketplace** — farmers borrow 70–75% of mandi price, disbursed in 24 hours via 11 partner banks/NBFCs
4. **AI "Sell Signal"** — recommends optimal sale timing, connects farmers directly to buyers

**Why nobody has solved this:** it requires running physical warehouse operations, blockchain/regulatory-compliant registry infrastructure, and a bank-grade lending marketplace all at once — most players attempt only one. Because every loan is collateral-backed by verified physical stock, credit risk is structurally lower than unsecured farmer lending — and loans against Annapurna's own warehouses run at roughly half the NPA rate of partner-warehouse loans, since Annapurna directly controls custody and quality.
