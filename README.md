**\# Annapurna Capital Technologies — Series B Financial Model & Valuation**

### Corporate Finance & Valuation Portfolio Project

A comprehensive corporate finance case study built around **Annapurna Capital Technologies Pvt. Ltd.**, a fictional Indian agri-fintech focused on post-harvest financing through a hybrid network of partner warehouses and owned mini-warehouses.

The project evaluates the company's operating model, five-year financial outlook, capital requirements and potential Series B valuation through an integrated financial model, valuation analysis, market-sizing assessment and investor pitchbook.

> **Important:** Annapurna Capital Technologies is a fictional company created specifically for this portfolio project. The historical operating data, business assumptions and transaction details are case-study assumptions and should not be interpreted as real company information.

---

## Project Overview

Annapurna operates a hybrid warehouse model combining:

- **Partner warehouses** — third-party warehouses equipped with IoT verification infrastructure.
- **Owned mini-warehouses** — smaller, modular warehouses built directly by Annapurna in underserved rural markets.
- **Digital financing marketplace** — connects farmers seeking post-harvest financing with partner banks/NBFCs.
- **Warehouse SaaS / IoT infrastructure** — generates recurring technology-related revenue from partner warehouses.
- **Marketplace transaction fees** — revenue linked to agricultural GMV facilitated through the platform.
- **Storage revenue** — generated from Annapurna-owned mini-warehouses.
- **AI-based sell signal** — intended to help farmers identify better timing for selling stored commodities.

The case assumes a **₹140 Cr Series B raise**, primarily allocated toward warehouse expansion, technology development, operating scale-up and working capital.

---

## Project Objectives

The objective was to build an integrated corporate-finance package answering five key questions:

1. **How does the business grow over the next five years?**
2. **Does the operating model translate into sustainable profitability and cash generation?**
3. **What is the company's intrinsic and market-based valuation?**
4. **How large is the addressable market and what constraints affect market capture?**
5. **What valuation and dilution would be appropriate for a ₹140 Cr Series B?**

The project covers:

- Driver-based financial forecasting
- Three-statement modelling
- DCF valuation
- Comparable-company analysis
- TAM/SAM/SOM analysis
- Series B valuation
- Dilution and cap-table analysis
- Investor pitchbook

# Financial Model

The core of the project is a **driver-based five-year financial model covering FY27E–FY31E**.

The financial model was independently built in Excel, with operating assumptions flowing through the financial statements and ultimately into the valuation.
